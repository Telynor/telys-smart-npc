# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release for Foundry v14, D&D5e 5.3.3, and midi-QOL's v14 activity API.

- GM-reviewed turn plans with token, path, and target previews.
- Native pathfinding with bounded A* fallback and prepared movement budgets.
- Automatic item activities, attacks, saves, and damage through midi-QOL.
- HSR resource/weakness/stance awareness and special-turn safeguards.
- Player reaction requests, GM approval, Shield/AC/save timing, and replanning.
- Per-token tactics, multiattack sequences, healing, area targeting, and spell slots.
- 58 automated tests. Live Foundry validation remains pending.
